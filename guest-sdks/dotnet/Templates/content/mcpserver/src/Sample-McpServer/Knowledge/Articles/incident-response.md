# Incident response

An incident is anything customers notice that we did not plan.

1. Say so in the incidents channel. The first person to do that is the incident
   lead until they hand over.
2. Stop the harm before finding the cause: roll back the last deploy, switch
   off the feature, or fail over.
3. Post an update every thirty minutes, even when nothing has changed.
4. Within a week, hold a review that asks what made the mistake easy, not who
   made it.
